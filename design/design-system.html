Foundations

Three principles.

The same ideas that anchor Apple's Human Interface Guidelines — applied to a product whose best interface is almost no interface at all.

01

Clarity

Type is legible at every size, icons are precise, and the accent color is reserved for what matters. Negative space does the heavy lifting.

02

Deference

The UI steps back so your words come forward. Fluid motion and a crisp, unobtrusive chrome keep the focus on content, never on the frame.

03

Depth

Layered materials and realistic motion convey hierarchy and help you understand where you are — without a single line of instruction.

Color

Ink, off-white, chrome.

A strictly monochrome palette — no accent color. Emphasis comes from ink, chrome, and true black; actions are carried by weight and contrast, never hue.

Motion

Physical, not flashy.

Every transition uses Apple's standard ease cubic-bezier(.28,.11,.32,1). Springs for arrival, fades for change, gentle rise for reveal. Respects Reduce Motion.
