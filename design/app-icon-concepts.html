Give freeflo a Mac app icon

The Dock & Finder icon today is a generic logo-maker placeholder — it shares nothing with the locked brand. Eight replacements below, all built from the same waveform mark already used in the menu bar and on the website. Click a card to shortlist it; your picks are saved at the bottom of the page.

Brand mark · menu bar & site The locked "A" mark — living waveform chip, chrome bars on near-black. The app icon should read as its sibling, not a stranger.
01 · default✓

Equalizer

The locked waveform mark, scaled to fill the icon. Instantly recognizable from the menu bar and the website — the safe, correct default.

02 · radial pulse✓

Radial Pulse

Sound radiating from a center point — an "always listening" indicator rather than a waveform. Reads more like a badge; more circular than the chip.

03 · waveform line✓

Waveform Line

One continuous audio trace instead of discrete bars — softer and more organic, closer to "flo" (flow) than "eq."

04 · monogram✓

Monogram Bars

Three chrome bars form the letter F — dual-codes the waveform language with the wordmark's initial. The most brand-recognizable option with zero text.

05 · aperture✓

Aperture Ring

A lens / record-dot reading — camera-and-mic capsule language for "always listening." The calmest, most restrained option.

06 · flow✓

Flow Chevrons

Brand mark "B," scaled up. Leads with momentum/forward motion over voice — use if "flo" should outrank "free" in the read.

07 · tribar✓

Minimal Tribar

The equalizer reduced to three bars. Built for legibility — the most confident read at 16px, in the Dock and in Finder.

08 · halo✓

Halo Bars

Equalizer bars set inside a thin ring. More emblematic, closer to a seal or badge — a touch more formal than 01.
