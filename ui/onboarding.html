Voice, on-device

Talk to your machine. The words appear.

freeflo turns speech into text anywhere on your Mac — hold a key, speak, release. Transcription runs entirely on-device. No cloud, no API keys, nothing leaves this machine.

System access

Two permissions to work.

macOS gates the microphone and keyboard for your safety. Grant these once and freeflo can listen and type at your cursor.

Microphone

Capture audio while you hold the key. Recording is temporary — deleted the instant it's transcribed.

Accessibility

Detect your global hotkey and paste text into the app you're using.

A RESTART MAY BE NEEDED AFTER GRANTING
Opens System Settings → Privacy & Security. freeflo never reads your screen or other apps.
