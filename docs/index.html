freeflo

Hold a key. Speak. It types.

freeflo is a free dictation app for Mac. Your speech becomes text on your Mac, not on a server.

Download for Mac

v1.5.0 · freeflo-macos26.zip · 22 MB

Needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Older Mac? Get v1.4.3.

How it works

  1. Hold Left Option. In any app, wherever you can type.
  2. Speak. A small indicator on screen shows freeflo is listening.
  3. Let go. Your words are typed at the cursor about 0.2 seconds later.

For longer dictation, tap Right Option to start and tap it again to stop. Esc cancels.

Works in the languages your Mac can transcribe. You can mix Hindi and English in one sentence; Hindi is written in English letters.

Why it's safe

  • Your voice stays on your Mac. The speech recognition built into macOS turns it into text. Audio is never saved and never uploaded.
  • Your text stays on your Mac. History is a file on your Mac. Turn it off in Settings with "Keep my history".
  • No account and no tracking. This version sends no usage data and no crash reports. Settings has two switches, "Share anonymous usage" and "Send crash reports". In this version they do nothing, and you can turn them off.
  • One thing goes online by default. freeflo reads the public release list on GitHub to check for updates, when it opens and every 6 hours. It sends nothing about you.
  • Two permissions, one job each. Microphone, to hear you while you dictate. Accessibility, to notice the hotkey and type the text.
  • Open source. Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read it or build it yourself.

Two optional downloads, each fetched once and then used on your Mac: extra languages, which come from Apple, and Turbo, an off-by-default feature that tidies your text using a small model downloaded from Hugging Face.

Install

  1. Download and open the zip.
  2. Drag freeflo to Applications and open it.
  3. Allow Microphone and Accessibility in the setup window.

If macOS won't open it

Apple has not reviewed freeflo, so macOS may refuse to open it. To allow it, open Terminal (press Command and Space, type Terminal, press Return), paste this line and press Return. Then open freeflo again.

xattr -r -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/freeflo.app
